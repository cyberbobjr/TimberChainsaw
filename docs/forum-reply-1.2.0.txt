Thanks for the report, and sorry about your two characters. You were right on all three points. I checked the numbers against the game code:

- Damage: the running chainsaw hit softer than a regular axe (0.6-1.1 vs 0.8-2.0).
- Stamina: the chainsaw weighs 7 and keeps attacking every 0.67 s while you hold the button, so it drained endurance about 2 to 3 times faster than an axe.
- Noise: it attracted zombies from 50 tiles every 2 seconds, even when idling.

Version 1.2.0 is out:
- Damage raised to 1.2-2.4, more than the axe and less than the wood axe. The Damage sandbox option now goes up to 500 %.
- New "Endurance cost" sandbox option, 50 % by default (100 % = old behaviour, 0 % = no endurance cost).
- Noise is now split into two options: idle (20 tiles by default) and attacking or cutting (40 tiles by default).

Existing saves get the new defaults automatically, and every value can be changed in the sandbox options (Chainsaw B42 page). One thing it still won't do is knock zombies down: the game decides that through the zombie's hit animation, not through a weapon value, so keep some space when you rev it up.
