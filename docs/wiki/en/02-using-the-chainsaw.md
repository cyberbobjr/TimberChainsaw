## Two states

| Item | Engine | In combat |
|---|---|---|
| **Chainsaw** | stopped | a heavy, weak club (0.1 to 0.5 damage) |
| **Chainsaw (running)** | running | 1.2 to 2.4 damage, 25 % critical hits |

Both weigh **7** and have **127** condition points. The chainsaw is a two-handed weapon.

## Start

1. Right-click the chainsaw in your inventory (or anywhere in the world while holding it): **Start the chainsaw**.
2. It is equipped in both hands if needed, then your character pulls the starter.

The option is greyed out, with the reason in its tooltip, when the chainsaw is broken, its tank is empty, or you are in a vehicle.

**Starting failures**: below half condition, a chainsaw has a 25 % chance (option) of not starting ("*Sputter* The chainsaw won't start..."). Try again, or repair it.

> Capture à faire : the context menu with "Start the chainsaw".

## Fight

- Aim with the right mouse button and attack with the left button, like any melee weapon.
- Hold the attack button: the chainsaw stays pointed forward at full throttle and hits what is in front of you every 0.67 s.
- Each zombie killed costs 20 condition points (option). The endurance cost is half that of a normal 7 kg weapon (option).
- Zombies are not knocked down by the chainsaw.

> Capture à faire : a running chainsaw cutting through a zombie.

## Stop

Right-click: **Stop the chainsaw**. The engine also stops by itself when:

- the chainsaw leaves your hands (put in a bag, a crate, on the ground);
- the tank runs dry ("The chainsaw ran out of fuel") or the chainsaw breaks ("The chainsaw broke");
- you get in a vehicle or die;
- you reload the game or reconnect: engines do not survive a reload.

Starting and stopping keep the fuel, condition, repair count, favourite mark, blood and custom name.

## Noise

A running engine attracts zombies: a 20-tile noise every 2 seconds when idling, and 40 tiles while attacking or cutting (options).
